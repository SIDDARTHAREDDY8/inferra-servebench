Saw your HN post. You're bringing up GPU datacenters and pushing hardware fungibility, and there's one thing that bites every neocloud doing that: serving configs don't transfer. The vLLM flags tuned on an H100 will thrash a 24GB card's KV cache, so each new SKU means another round of manual tuning during bring-up.

I built a small harness for exactly that problem. It sweeps serving configs (tensor parallel, max_num_seqs, batched tokens, KV util, chunked prefill, prefix caching) across GPU SKUs and ranks them by throughput-per-GPU plus a fungibility score, so you can pick one config that degrades least everywhere instead of retuning per SKU. It benchmarks any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, ships with a mock engine and test server so the measurement path is validated, and includes a runbook for producing real numbers on your nodes.
